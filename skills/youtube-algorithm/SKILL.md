# YouTube Algorithm — design every video for it

**One rule:** the system does not rank videos, it predicts *which viewer will be satisfied by which video*. It tests a video on a small
matching audience, measures what they do, and widens or stops. So: make the first viewers stay, finish, and want another one.

## SHORTS FEED — what is measured, in order of weight
| Signal | What it means | Design answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewed vs swiped away** | did they stay past the first ~1–2 s | motion + payoff image + hook words on frame 1; no logo, no intro, no slow push |
| **Average % viewed** (loops count, >100% possible) | did they finish / rewatch | payoff-first then rewind, a beat every 2–3 s, no dead second, seamless loop ending |
| **Engagement** (likes, shares, comments, follows from the Short) | satisfaction | one honest comment question, a shareable "send this to…" fact, series hook |
| **Negative** (swipe in 1 s, "not interested", mismatch title↔video) | kills the test | never bait; the first frame must match what the video delivers |

Targets: **≥ 70% viewed-vs-swiped** is healthy, **80–87%** is the goal · **avg % viewed ≥ 90%** on 25–35 s, **> 100%** with a loop.
- **Length:** Shorts may be up to 3 min (vertical/square). Shorter finishes better: 25–35 s default; 45–60 s only if every second earns it;
  60 s+ only for a real story with a re-hook every 5–8 s. Never pad.
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 9:16, 30 or 60 fps, sound on frame 1, loudness ≈ −14 LUFS (YouTube normalises to it).
- **Safe zone (UI covers the rest):** keep words out of the top 8%, the bottom 22% and the right 15% of the lower half (buttons); captions at y ≈ 0.70–0.76.
- **Sound-off + sound-on:** captions the whole way AND a voice/sound hit on frame 1.
- **Title** (≤ 100 allowed; first ~40 chars are what shows): the hook in other words + the searchable topic. **Hashtags ≤ 3** (topic ones;
  `#shorts` is optional). **Related video:** link every Short to the matching long video/next part (funnel to watch time).
- **Cover frame** is picked in the mobile app at upload — choose the payoff frame; it matters on the channel page and in search, not in the feed.
- **Views:** every play/replay counts as a view; *engaged views* are what monetisation uses — judge a Short by viewed-vs-swiped and % viewed, not raw views.
- **Niche consistency:** the feed learns an audience from a run of similar videos. Same niche, same format, same look, daily or every other day.

## LONG-FORM (16:9) — two gates
1. **The click (packaging):** title + thumbnail are one idea, made BEFORE the script. CTR is judged against how widely it is shown —
   a broad home-feed video at 4–6% can beat a niche one at 10%. Thumbnail = 1 face/emotion + 1 object + ≤ 3 words, readable at phone size,
   and it must NOT repeat the title (they complete each other). Use **Test & Compare** (3 thumbnails; winner = watch-time share).
2. **The watch (satisfaction):** average view duration and % viewed, plus likes / "not interested" / survey answers.
   - **First 30 s decide the video:** second 0 confirms the title+thumbnail promise, no intro/logo/"welcome back"; target **≥ 70% still
     watching at 0:30**, **≥ 50% average viewed**, a flat retention line (dips = cut that part next time, spikes = do more of it).
   - Open a loop in the cold open and close it at the END; a new loop every chapter; re-hook every 30–60 s; pattern break every 3–6 s.
   - **Length = as long as the story holds.** 8–12 min is the sweet spot for depth + mid-roll ads (≥ 8 min); never stretch to hit it.
   - **Chapters** in the description (first `0:00`, ≥ 3, each ≥ 10 s, keyword names) → key moments in search.
   - **Ending:** no "thanks for watching" (viewers leave the moment they hear a goodbye). Last line hands off to the next video;
     end screen in the last 5–20 s pointing at ONE best next video + playlist. Session time counts for you.
   - **Upload at 1440p+** when possible (better codec on YouTube), 16:9, −14 LUFS.

## IDEA FIRST (80% of the result)
- A video is only as good as its premise: *curiosity gap + clear stakes + a visual the viewer has never seen*. Write 10 premises, keep 1.
- Proven angles for 3D: what-if, inside-the-body, survival, true crazy stories, dangerous jobs, history myths, "how it really works".
- **Title test:** would a stranger click this with no channel name on it? ≤ 60 chars, concrete noun, number or stake, no clickbait lie.
- Search + browse: put the real topic words in the title's first half and the description's first line (that is the metadata that matters;
  **tags barely matter** — use them for misspellings/alternate names only).
- **Series beat one-offs:** same format, new topic ("You vs ___", "Day 1 in ___"); number parts only when a story truly continues.

## UPLOAD CHECKLIST (goes in every publish pack)
Title · description (line 1 = hook + topic words; chapters for long-form) · ≤ 3 hashtags · thumbnail / cover frame · playlist ·
related video (Shorts) · end screen + cards (long) · pinned comment (the comment question) · language + captions file if available ·
audience: **not made for kids** (unless it truly is) · **altered-content disclosure**: tick it for realistic-looking synthetic scenes of
real people/places/events; clearly animated/stylised 3D does not need it — when unsure, tick it · no reused/other-platform watermarks.
Monetisation safety: every video must add original story/voice/visuals — never mass-produce near-identical template videos.

## AFTER PUBLISHING — the loop (ask Sir for the numbers, or read .motion-forge/state.md "CHANNEL DATA")
| Symptom | Cause | Fix in the next video |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts: most swipe in the first second | hook frame is weak/slow | stronger payoff image, motion + words on frame 1, cut the first 0.5 s |
| Shorts: stay, then leave at one time | a dead beat there | find that second; add a cut/re-hook earlier, shorten the setup |
| Shorts: good % viewed, few views | topic too narrow or off-niche | broader premise, same format |
| Long: low CTR | packaging | new title+thumbnail idea (not a tweak); test 3 |
| Long: high CTR, big drop by 0:30 | promise not confirmed at second 0 | open on the promised image, delete the intro |
| Long: slow steady decline | no open loops / monotone | add loops, re-hooks, visual variety, cut filler |
Double down on what the audience already watched: remake the best performer's format with a new topic before trying a new format.
