Michel Lotito ("Monsieur Mangetout"), Grenoble, France, 1950–2007.
- Ate a Cessna 150 light aircraft, cut into small pieces, 1978–1980 (about two years).
- Swallowed pieces with water (and mineral oil); widely reported ~900 g–1 kg of metal per day.
- Earlier/other items reported: 18 bicycles, 15 supermarket trolleys, 7 TV sets, 6 chandeliers, 2 beds, a pair of skis, a coffin.
- Doctors found his stomach and intestine walls about twice the normal thickness.
- Guinness World Records awarded him a brass plaque for "strangest diet", which he reportedly ate.
