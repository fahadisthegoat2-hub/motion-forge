**Description**
In 1978, Soviet physicist Anatoli Bugorski leaned into the path of the U-70 particle accelerator's proton beam in Protvino.
It entered the back of his skull and came out by his nose. He saw a flash "brighter than a thousand suns" and felt no pain. Doctors expected him to die — he survived, finished his PhD and kept working at the same accelerator. True story, 3D animated (scenes dramatized).
