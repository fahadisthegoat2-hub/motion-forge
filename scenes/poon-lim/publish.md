Torpedoed in 1942, Poon Lim survived 133 days alone on a tiny raft in the South Atlantic — a record never beaten.
A ship sailed past and ignored him. He fished with a flashlight wire, drank rainwater, and walked off the raft on his own in Brazil. True story, 3D animated (scenes dramatized).
