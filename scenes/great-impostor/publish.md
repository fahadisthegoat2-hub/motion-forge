**Description**
He did surgery on a warship with zero medical training — and every patient lived.
In 1951, impostor Ferdinand Waldo Demara stole the identity of Dr. Joseph Cyr and joined HMCS Cayuga as its surgeon in the Korean War.
When wounded soldiers arrived, he speed-read a surgery textbook and operated — including removing a bullet from near a man's heart. His patients survived. The press called him a hero… and that's how the real Dr. Cyr found out. True story, 3D animated (scenes dramatized).
