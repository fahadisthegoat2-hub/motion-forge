FACTS (story-research) — The Fake Surgeon
- Ferdinand Waldo Demara Jr. (1921–1982), "The Great Impostor".
- 1951: used the credentials of Dr. Joseph C. Cyr (a real New Brunswick physician) to join the Royal Canadian Navy as a surgeon-lieutenant.
- Served aboard the destroyer HMCS Cayuga during the Korean War.
- Treated 16 wounded Korean combatants brought aboard; performed major surgery, including removing a bullet lodged near a patient's heart. Prepared by speed-reading a medical textbook. All patients survived.
- Navy press office publicised the story; the real Dr. Cyr's family/he saw the coverage → identity exposed; Navy dismissed him (no charges), deported to the US.
