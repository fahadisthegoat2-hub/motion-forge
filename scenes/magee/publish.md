**Description**
He fell 20,000 feet with no parachute — and a train station's glass roof saved his life.
True story: on 3 January 1943, US gunner Alan Magee's bomber was shot apart over Saint-Nazaire, France. He left the plane without a working parachute, blacked out, and crashed through the glass roof of the Saint-Nazaire railway station. Some scenes are dramatized.
